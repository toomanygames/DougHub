How to play

• Aim at the color cluster you want to hit.
• Match 3 or more bubbles of the same color to pop them.
• Floating bubbles not attached to the ceiling fall for bonus points.
• Every few shots, the ceiling drops lower. Don't let the bubbles reach you!
