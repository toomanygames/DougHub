HONK & ROLLTHE GOOSE GIG

Kicked out of the nest at 19, one goose, one dream, and a very loud beak. Beat thirteen rivals in music battles, from a banjo frog to a vampire duck to the Milkman himself.

How to play

Every level is a music battle. Your rival plays a phrase first: watch the notes and listen. Then it is your turn: honk the same notes as they reach the glowing line. The crowd cheers for whoever plays best.

Keys: A / S / D, or ← ↓ →, or J / K / L. Touch: tap the three big buttons. Perfect timing scores the most, and long combos add a bonus.
