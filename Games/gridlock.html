GRIDLOCK

A tiny logic puzzle with a sneaky goal: light up every target cell. Each tap flips that cell and its direct neighbors. Find the sequence that turns the whole board green.

How to play
Each move flips the selected tile plus the tiles above, below, left, and right. Turn all 25 tiles green. Hints reveal a tile from the original solution. Puzzles are generated from a solvable board, so every level has an answer.
