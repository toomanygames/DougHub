1. Weekly IP address log

When you open the DougHub app, it records a short visit entry. We do this to keep DougHub safe, for example to spot abuse, spam or people who come back after being banned. It is not used for advertising or for tracking what you do.

Each entry contains:

  • your IP address
  • the date and time
  • your browser type (the user agent text your device sends)
  • your account, only if you are signed in
  • the word "app", which says where the visit came from

An entry is saved at most once a week for each device and for each IP address, so opening the app many times in a week still makes one entry. Only the owner of DougHub can read or delete these entries. The entries are not sold, not shared, and not sent outside the DougHub database.

3. Who can see it

What you post in chat, the forum, DougTube or your showcase is visible to other members. Private information, such as the visit log and your account details, is visible only to DougHub staff who need it to run the site, and the visit log only to the owner. We do not sell your information and we do not give it to other companies to use.

4. Where it is stored

DougHub runs on a hosted database and web hosting service. Your information is stored in that database. Those services handle it only to run DougHub for us.

5. Your choices

  • You can sign up with a username and no email.
  • You can change your username, picture and settings in Settings.
  • You can ask us to delete your account or to delete your visit log entries. Use the Report a problem page and say what you want removed.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. By using DougHub you agree to this Privacy Policy and to the Terms of Service.
