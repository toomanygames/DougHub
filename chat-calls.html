Call tips
  • Microphone: green means on; dark means muted. If nobody hears you, use the mic button to retry.
  • Camera: green means on; dark means off. You can still talk with the camera off.
  • Sound: use the speaker-shaped volume button to adjust each participant locally.
  • Invite: use the people button to select DougHub users or copy the room link.
  • Connection: everyone should join the same room link. If audio fails, leave and rejoin.
  • Privacy: leaving the page ends your camera and microphone session.
