Call room

Start a room to create an invite link. Anyone with the link can join until the room is full.

Before you start: allow camera and microphone access when your browser asks. Camera and microphone are requested separately, so camera problems should not stop your mic. Supports up to 6 people, but lower video quality may be needed on slower devices. Some networks block direct peer-to-peer media; a TURN relay is needed for those networks.
Your camera and microphone stream directly between participants. DougHub uses Supabase Realtime for room presence and connection setup; video itself is not uploaded to DougHub. Share room links only with people you want in the call.
