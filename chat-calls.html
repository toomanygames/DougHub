Call room

Start a call to create an invite link. The person you invite can join from any modern browser.

Before you start: allow camera and microphone access when your browser asks. This first version supports one-to-one calls. Connection depends on your network; some networks may block direct peer-to-peer connections.
Your camera and microphone stream directly between participants. DougHub uses Supabase Realtime to exchange connection setup messages; video itself is not uploaded to DougHub.
