GETTING STARTED

Your classroom, all in one place.

A shared space for class chat, learning tools, reminders, and games. Open your class chat, share ideas, and use the student tools.

For students

Join your class and use its learning tools.

STUDENT
1
Browse and request to join

Open the Class directory, choose an available class, and send a join request.

2
Wait for your teacher

Your teacher reviews the request. After approval, the Class link opens directly into that classroom.

3
Open your classroom

Use the classroom selector if you belong to more than one class. Your teacher must be online for the room to open.

4
Use the class tools

Chat with your class, check reminders, look up words, use the focus timer, and play the classroom games.

5
When the room is closed

If your teacher is offline or has paused the session, wait until the room becomes available again. The page checks for the teacher automatically.

Classroom basics

Important rules for everyone.

  • Classrooms are private to approved students, their teacher, and authorized DougHub staff.
  • Keep class chat kind, relevant, and respectful.
  • Teachers manage reminders and classroom word filters.
  • Developers and higher staff ranks can preview the student experience without joining a roster; this does not grant teacher controls.
  • Never share passwords or private information in class chat.
