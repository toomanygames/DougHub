GETTING STARTED

Welcome to DougHub Classroom.

A shared space for class chat, learning tools, reminders, and games. Choose the guide that matches your role.

GRADES 4–8PRIVATE CLASS SPACESTEACHER-MANAGED

For teachers

Set up a class and invite your students.

TEACHER
1
Get teacher access

Open Teacher Studio and request verification. Classroom creation unlocks after the DougHub Owner approves your teacher account. Owners can create classes immediately.

2
Create your classroom

Choose a class name and grade from 4 through 8, then select Create classroom. Your class appears in the list on the left.

3
Request student enrollment

Students find your class on the Classroom page and send a join request. Accept or reject requests from your teacher roster; no Owner approval is needed for student enrollment.

4
Manage the class

Post reminders, review class chat, manage the word filter, and use the session controls when needed.

5
Keep the room available

The classroom is open while your DougHub account is online. If you go offline, students see Classroom Closed. Return online to reopen access.

Open Teacher Studio

For students

Join your class and use its learning tools.

STUDENT
1
Ask your teacher to add you

Give your teacher your DougHub username so they can send an enrollment request.

2
Wait for approval

Your teacher reviews the request. The class appears automatically after your teacher accepts it.

3
Open your classroom

Use the classroom selector if you belong to more than one class. Your teacher must be online for the room to open.

4
Use the class tools

Chat with your class, check reminders, look up words, use the focus timer, and play the classroom games.

5
When the room is closed

If your teacher is offline or has paused the session, wait until the room becomes available again. The page checks for the teacher automatically.

Classroom basics

Important rules for everyone.

  • Classrooms are private to approved students, their teacher, and authorized DougHub staff.
  • Keep class chat kind, relevant, and respectful.
  • Teachers manage reminders and classroom word filters.
  • Developers and higher staff ranks can preview the student experience without joining a roster; this does not grant teacher controls.
  • Never share passwords or private information in class chat.
