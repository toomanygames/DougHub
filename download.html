Questions

Do I need the app to use DougHub?

No. The website works in any browser. The app is a separate Windows program with its own games, shop and leaderboards, and it needs to be installed.

Is it a different account?

No. Use the same username or email and password you use on the website.

I have no email. Can I still sign up?

Yes. Choose a username and password and leave the email empty. Without an email, a forgotten password can't be reset.

How do I update?

Download the newest installer from this page and run it. Your account and progress stay on DougHub.
